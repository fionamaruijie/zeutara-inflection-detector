Vellum raises $14M Series A to build the revenue operating layer for B2B SaaS

Vellum today announced it has raised a $14M Series A round led by Threshold Ventures, with participation from existing seed investors. The round will be used to expand the engineering and revenue teams and accelerate the platform's enterprise tier. Vellum closed its seed round of $4.2M just over twelve months ago, also led by Threshold.

Who we are

Vellum was founded in 2023 by Maya Park and Daniel Reyes. Today we are a team of 38 people across New York and remote. Our customers include mid-market SaaS companies in finance, logistics and developer tools. We are pursuing the enterprise tier of the market, where deal review and forecasting accuracy compound directly into ARR.

Maya, our CEO, still owns enterprise sales today. As we move up-market we are building the team to support that motion — see our careers page.

Built for revenue leaders

Vellum serves B2B SaaS teams between 30 and 500 employees. Our largest customers are Fortune 1000 logistics and infrastructure companies who use Vellum to run their internal sales operating cadence.
