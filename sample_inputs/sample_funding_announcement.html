Vellum Raises $14M Series A to Build the Revenue Operating Layer for B2B SaaS

By Tech Press Wire — March 24, 2026

NEW YORK — Vellum, the revenue operating platform for mid-market and enterprise B2B SaaS, today announced it has closed a $14M Series A round led by Threshold Ventures. Threshold partner Renee Cho will join Vellum's board.

The round comes thirteen months after Vellum's $4.2M seed, also led by Threshold. The company says the new capital will be used to:

  • Hire its first Head of Sales, with a focus on enterprise account-executive hiring underneath.
  • Stand up a Revenue Operations function to support the move up-market.
  • Build out the enterprise tier of the platform, including custom roles and SAML SSO.

Vellum's founder and CEO Maya Park acknowledged that she has owned enterprise sales personally since founding, and said the Series A was timed deliberately around the operating shift: "We are not just hiring a Head of Sales. We are installing the architecture they need to succeed before they start. The team is forty people today and we expect to be around seventy by year end."

Vellum's existing customers include several mid-market and Fortune 1000 logistics and infrastructure companies. The company serves B2B SaaS revenue teams between roughly 30 and 500 employees.
